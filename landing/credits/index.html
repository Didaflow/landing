Children’s Games, 1560

Pieter Bruegel the Elder. Oil on panel. Collection of the Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna.

Digital reproduction sourced from Wikimedia Commons / Google Arts & Culture. The source identifies the painting and reproduction with a Public Domain Mark.

View the artwork source ↗

Decorative figures are adapted from the original painting using AI-assisted background extraction and asset preparation. Gentle movements respond to scrolling. A fragment of the original painting and interacting groups create the opening composition. Illustrations are confined to the opening scene and stakeholder question panels. The painting is not modified to represent interventions or outcomes.
