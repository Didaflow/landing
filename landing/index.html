Our story

Loves me, loves me not.

Pluck a daisy, pull the petals one by one — loves me, loves me not — and let the last one decide. A coin toss dressed as a flower. Educational institutions settle real decisions much the same way: which course to grow, which cohort to rescue, which intervention to fund — left to instinct and the petal that falls last.

Why a flower

didaflowers turns that daisy around. Ours doesn't take a petal from you — it gives you one. Each petal is evidence, drawn from your own enrolments, exams, and graduate careers: one clear answer, traceable to where it grew from.

Where we took root

We started in 2025 in Bologna, inside the University of Bologna research ecosystem — founded by academics tired of watching good institutions decide by feel. The rigour they demand of research, finally turned on the institution.

Three lenses

Every cohort, three questions.

Didaflow watches each cohort through three Outcome lenses — each tied to ANVUR indicators you already report on. Drill into one, and the others stay in context.

Attractiveness

Are applicants showing up — and from where? Track demand across regions, school types, and entry channels.
