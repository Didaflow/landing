Method

Machine in the Loop

Decisions stay with people

Teachers and experts set the questions and the hypotheses. Didaflow turns them into a model, simulates the consequences and shows the evidence behind them.

Read the methodology ↗

  1. Hypotheses come from experts

    Data are used to test and refine them.

  2. Everything can be checked

    Assumptions, sources and uncertainty are visible. The model can be challenged and revised.

  3. Interventions are compared

    We estimate what happens if the institution changes something, before it does.

In practice

Two projects with real institutions

Designed with the people who work there. Names are confidential.

A public university

Monitoring programmes and understanding dropout

The question

How can an institution bring fragmented data together to monitor its programmes, understand dropout and support educational decisions?

The implementation
A monitoring platform integrates institutional data. A proprietary causal model, co-built with domain experts and educational scientists, enables what-if simulations within the same platform.
Current status
Platform ready. The work connects programme monitoring to the exploration of interventions.

A business school

A student assistant and tools for staff

The question

How can fragmented institutional knowledge support students and decision-makers while keeping educational responsibility with the school?

The implementation
A contextualised student assistant uses curated materials and is co-designed with stakeholders. Dedicated agents support educators, coordinators and administrators, using a co-built causal model to explore the impact of interventions.
Current status
Tools ready. A shared data foundation supports both learning and institutional decisions.

Training and co-design

People learn the tools by designing them with us

A new tool works only if the people who use it understand it. So training is part of the project: we design each tool with the teachers, coordinators, leaders and students who will use it, and while they shape it they learn how to use it and when not to trust it. We also offer consulting and training on AI in teaching and governance.
