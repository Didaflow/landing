Our story

Loves me, loves me not.

Pluck a daisy, pull the petals one by one — loves me, loves me not — and let the last one decide. A coin toss dressed as a flower. Universities settle real decisions much the same way: which course to grow, which cohort to rescue, which intervention to fund — left to instinct and the petal that falls last.

Why a flower

didaflowers turns that daisy around. Ours doesn't take a petal from you — it gives you one. Each petal is evidence, drawn from your own enrolments, exams, and graduate careers: one clear answer, traceable to where it grew from.

Where we took root

We started in 2025 in Bologna, inside the University of Bologna research ecosystem — founded by academics tired of watching good institutions decide by feel. The rigour they demand of research, finally turned on the institution.

Three lenses
