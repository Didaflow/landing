In practice

Real institutional questions
Shared work

Two implementations, co-designed with the people who know their institution. Names remain confidential.

A public university

Understanding programme health

+

The question

How can an institution bring fragmented data together to monitor its programmes, understand dropout and support educational decisions?

The implementation

A monitoring platform integrates institutional data. A proprietary causal model, co-built with domain experts and educational scientists, enables what-if simulations within the same platform.

Current status

Platform ready. The work connects programme monitoring to the exploration of interventions.

A business school

Institutional knowledge, with intent

+

The question

How can fragmented institutional knowledge support students and decision-makers while keeping educational responsibility with the school?

The implementation

A contextualised student assistant uses curated materials and is co-designed with stakeholders. Dedicated agents support educators, coordinators and administrators, using a co-built causal model to explore the impact of interventions.

Current status

Tools ready. A shared data foundation supports both learning and institutional decisions.
