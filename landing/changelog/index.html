Service Changelog

Monthly notes on Maurice, and the changelog for didaflow Studio, landing, and governance pages.

October 2026

What’s new in Maurice, October 2026

Maurice is the virtual assistant for students and faculty. This month it became more attentive and faster, and it is easier to use on your phone and on your computer.

Maurice keeps the whole conversation in mind

In a long conversation, Maurice no longer looks only at the latest messages: what you said at the start stays in context in later answers.

Questions about your course materials: faster, with sources

When you ask about readings, slides or the outline of your course, Maurice looks in your course materials first and answers more quickly. Under the answer you find the documents it used, so you can check them.

Maurice on your phone and on your computer

You can add it to your phone’s Home screen and open it like an app, without going through the app stores, or install it from the browser on your computer. On the phone, tabs at the bottom lead to Chat, Services and Files; the Wiki is coming in the next months.

Smoother chats

Switching conversations is faster, every chat gets a title as soon as you write the first message, and “Stop” keeps your question and the part of the answer already written. With chat search you can find a conversation by its title.

For faculty

When you ask Maurice to rethink the syllabus of your course, it first shows the data it has and the data that is missing, and proposes changes only afterwards. The suggestions under the chat are designed for those who teach, and the course selector shows the edition of each course.

Files: more reliable

The Files tab opens without errors and shows the status of each uploaded file. Very long PDFs uploaded in the chat are read using less memory.

April 29, 2026

Studio and shared design system consolidation

Studio is the only implemented application surface. Landing, legal pages, assets, icons, and future UI work now inherit from the shared design system under shared/design.

  • Moved shared brand assets out of landing-specific folders.
  • Removed the landing build step in favor of static HTML served locally by the lightweight dev server.
  • Removed Tailwind from Studio and kept the visual layer in plain CSS.
April 29, 2026

Studio analytics services

Studio focuses on decision-support workflows for academic governance teams.

  • CSV, PDF, and document ingestion for institutional analysis.
  • Dropout-risk graph endpoints and cohort-level exploratory reports.
  • Conversation history and analyst-style report generation for governance workflows.
April 29, 2026

Governance and compliance pages

Legal and trust pages now use the same layout language as the home page.

  • Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and Terms of Service share one coherent footer and legal CSS layer.
  • Added a dedicated Data Processing Addendum separate from the Privacy Policy.
  • Updated navigation so Studio points to didaflow.ai/studio.
