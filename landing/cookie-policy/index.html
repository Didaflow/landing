This site sets no cookies

didaflow.ai does not set cookies. It has no analytics, no advertising, no social plug-ins and no third-party scripts, so it shows no cookie banner. One external resource is loaded on every page: the Satoshi typeface used for headings, which your browser requests from Fontshare (Indestructible Type). That request reaches Fontshare’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser details like any web request. Other fonts and images come from our own server.

What the site saves in your browser

The AI Map (Commons) and its questionnaire use your browser’s local storage, a small space that belongs to this site. Reading pages, the home page and the legal pages save nothing.

commons.client

A random identifier, so you can change your own answers on the AI Map. We keep only a hash of it (see the Privacy Policy).

When it is created: The first time you send something to the AI Map (an answer, a usefulness vote, a report or an experience). Looking at the map does not create it.

How long it lasts: Until you delete it. It is sent only to the AI Map, with your contributions and to show you your own answers.

commons.own

The private keys for the experiences you published, so you can add updates to them.

When it is created: When you publish an experience.

How long it lasts: Until you delete it.

aimap-quiz

Your answers to the guided questionnaire, so you can resume it. It never leaves your browser.

When it is created: When you answer the first question.

How long it lasts: Until you restart the questionnaire, or delete it.

Managing and deleting it

You can delete these items at any time from your browser’s site-data settings (look for “Clear site data” or “Cookies and site data” for didaflow.ai). In a desktop browser you can also open the developer tools, then Application or Storage, then Local storage.

  • If you delete commons.client, you can no longer change the answers you gave before; new answers will use a new identifier.
  • If you delete commons.own, you can no longer add updates to the experiences you published. They stay on the map until deleted on request.
  • The questionnaire has a restart button; deleting aimap-quiz has the same effect.

To have contributions deleted from our database, see the Privacy Policy.

Legal basis

These items are technical: they are saved only to provide a function you asked for (changing your own answer, adding an update, resuming a questionnaire) and are not used to track you or for advertising, so they do not need your consent. What we receive when you contribute is described in the Privacy Policy.
