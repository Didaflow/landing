Commons and the AI Map

Contributing to the AI Map needs no account, no email and no cookies. When you contribute we store:

  • Answers: whether your experience matches the research for a task, and whether an analysis was useful, with the role you chose.
  • Experiences and replies: rating, role, level, field, group size, how you used the tool, what you observed, your text and, if you give one, a display name.
  • Reports: tasks, tools or studies you tell us are missing.
  • Reports of contributions: counted once per browser and reviewed by a moderator; nothing is hidden automatically.
  • Usage counters: daily totals of which roles, tasks and tools were opened, without any identifier.

Your browser keeps a random identifier, so you can change your own answers, and a private key for each experience you publish, so you can add how it went. We store only a one-way hash of the identifier. Your IP address is used only to limit abuse: it is combined with a key that changes every day, kept in memory and never written to our database. Standard web server logs are described under retention.

Contributions are stored on Didaflow’s servers in the EU and are not shared with third parties. Experiences and replies are published under CC BY 4.0 when you choose to publish them; please do not include personal data about other people. We use answers, experiences and counters to improve the map and to study where practice and research agree, always in aggregate.

Legal basis: consent for published contributions (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); legitimate interest for answers, reports and counters (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). To remove a contribution, write to privacy@didaflow.com with its date and text.
